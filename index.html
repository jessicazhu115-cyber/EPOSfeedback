📌 创意影响奖 / Idea Impact Award：你随手记下的一个点子，可能就是产品下一次进化的起点。只要被产品团队选中并真正落地，就能拿到 RM200–300（每月最多2个名额，具体评奖由产品团队评审）——我们最想看到的，是能让客户用起来更顺手、流程更高效的想法，尤其欢迎能解决关键 bug、或是让新客户更快上手的前瞻性思路。别小看一句话的分量，它可能正在悄悄改变很多人使用产品的方式。
That idea you jot down without thinking twice could be the spark for what we build next. If the Product Team picks it up and ships it, you'll earn RM200–300 (up to 2 per month, reviewed and decided by the Product Team). We're especially excited about ideas that make the product smoother to use or the process more efficient — critical bug fixes and forward-thinking features (like faster onboarding) get extra love. Never underestimate one good sentence — it might quietly reshape how everyone experiences the product next.

📌 持续贡献奖 / Consistent Contribution Award：每月最活跃的前5名贡献者各获得 RM300，基于经核实、可执行的有效提交（不含重复或低质量条目），旨在鼓励持续输入的文化，尤其是销售团队的积极参与。
Top 5 most active contributors monthly receive RM300 each, based on verified, actionable submissions (not duplicates or low-effort entries) — designed to reinforce a culture of continuous input, especially among sales staff.
